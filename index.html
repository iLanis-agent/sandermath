The grit truth

Skip a rung and the scratches from the last grit outlive the finish. From your start grit to your finish grit, the honest ladder is every rung between them, named one by one - two honest passes, a working progression, or a long ladder, honest every rung.

The disc truth

Coarse grit eats discs faster than fine (labeled sqft-per-disc norms). Job square feet divided by the norm is the honest disc count - a one-disc job, a working sleeve, or buy the box, not the sleeve.

The afternoon truth

Passes times square feet at the 0.8 square-feet-a-minute norm (labeled) is the honest clock on the job - done before the dust settles, a working afternoon, or a two-day finish. The box's minutes were measured on someone else's bookcase.

What it never does

It never skips a rung on the ladder, never counts a worn disc as a working one, never schedules the passes out of the afternoon, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the last pass at dusk keeps the honest ledger.

Sander Math is illustrative arithmetic with labeled norms, not a measurement of your sander, your discs or your project. Follow the manual's safety instructions.
